# C4.1c event integrations and job handlers verification

Verified: 2026-09-05

## Delivered capability

- Dependency-free RFC 5545 calendar output for attendee invitations, cancellations, series and
  public feeds, with stable event identity and UTF-8-safe line folding.
- A configuration-backed Zoom client with HTTPS-only endpoints, bounded timeouts, stable request
  and result objects, and errors that do not retain credentials, tokens or provider URLs.
- Optional banner, write-up, recording-processor and recording-ready hooks with validated output.
- Durable reminder planning and delivery, pending-verification expiry, Zoom synchronization and
  recording-processing handlers with scalar-only payloads.
- Stable enqueue keys for Zoom and recording jobs.
- A durable Zoom mutation fence that prevents a retry from repeating a provider mutation after an
  indeterminate worker failure.
- Scheduled reminder planning and pending-verification expiry.

The `events` migrations remain provisional and untagged.
