# C4.1b registration, reminders and feedback verification

Verified: 2026-09-05

## Delivered capability

- One registration identity per event and normalized email, with an optional member owner.
- Confirmed authenticated registration with access checks and idempotent cancellation/reactivation.
- Standing series registration, future-occurrence fan-out and persistent per-occurrence opt-outs.
- Accountless free-event registration through pending verification, confirmation, cancellation and
  expiration, with attendance and no-show transitions.
- Version-scoped signed verification and management links generated only inside the mail worker.
- Durable mail intents with non-secret stored context and category suppression before job creation.
- Versioned reminder planning and claiming with scalar-only job inputs and terminal stale skips.
- Feedback creation and revision restricted to the owning confirmed registration after an event.
- After-commit registration signals alongside the event lifecycle signals from C4.1a.

The `events` migrations remain provisional and untagged.
